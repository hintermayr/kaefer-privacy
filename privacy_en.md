## What data does the app process?

- **Photos:** You take a photo or pick one from your gallery to have an insect identified. When *online recognition* is enabled, this image is sent via **Firebase AI Logic (Google)** to the **Google Gemini API** and is processed there solely for identification. We do **not permanently store** the image.
- **Offline recognition:** If online recognition is switched off, identification runs entirely **on your device** and no image ever leaves your device.
- **Settings:** Your "online recognition on/off" choice is stored only **locally on your device**.

The app collects **no** account, location, advertising or analytics data and contains **no** ads and **no** tracking.

## Recipients / third parties

**Google (Firebase AI Logic / Gemini API):** processes the submitted photo for image recognition. Note: when using the free Gemini tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its services. Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Gemini API terms additionally apply.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** Your device's camera app is opened to take the identification photo.
- **Internet / network access:** only to send the photo to the online service (only when online recognition is enabled).
- **Storage (Android 9 and below only):** optional, to copy captured photos to your gallery album.

## Legal basis

Processing the photo for identification takes place at your request and to provide the feature you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR).

## Retention

Photos are transmitted only for the duration of the identification and are not stored by us. Local settings remain on the device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.
